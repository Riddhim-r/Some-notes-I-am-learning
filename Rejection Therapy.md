A rejection challenge suits you because you can treat it as one of your psychology experiments on yourself. It's basically exposure therapy, and journalling makes it measurable.

**How to play:** you score a point for every no. A yes is a bonus, but it doesn't add to your count. Aim for one ask a day, or set a goal of 100 no's. When someone says no, ask "why not?" or "what would make it a yes?" Those follow-ups are where most of the learning happens. Write each ask in your journal with how nervous you felt (0–10) before and after. By about week 3 you should see that number drop.
