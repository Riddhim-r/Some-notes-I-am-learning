# Productive Identity — Bare Minimums Master List

Nov 4, 2026 · @Miss

## How to use this

Pick one identity (or a combo), do its core set every day for 2 weeks, then add one habit at a time. The master list has 196 bare minimums across 31 life disciplines, and each one takes 2–30 minutes. Numbers in the Identities and Combos sections point back to the master list.

- **Start small:** 5–7 habits a day beats 30 that last a week.
- **Bad-day rule:** on rough days, do Low Battery Mode (see Combos). The streak matters more than the size.
- **Times are in brackets**, so you can put each one on a timer.
- **Weekly items** are marked *(weekly)*. Do those once a week, not daily.
